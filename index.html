Frequently Asked Questions

What is GST and who needs to register?
GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a unified indirect tax. Registration is required if your annual turnover exceeds Rs 40 lakhs (or Rs 20 lakhs for certain states) or if you make interstate supplies.
How often do I need to file GST returns?
Quarterly filers submit GSTR-1 (sales) and GSTR-3B (summary) every quarter. Monthly e-invoicing is required if turnover exceeds Rs 5 crores. Annual returns (GSTR-9) are filed once a year.
What documents do I need for company incorporation?
Typically: business structure decision, registered office address proof, identity and address proofs of directors/members, DIN (Director Identification Number) and DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) for filing.
What is a project report and when do I need it?
A project report is a detailed financial document prepared for bank loans, working capital limits or investor pitches. It includes projections, ratios, cash flow forecasts and highlights business viability.
Can you help with trust creation and Section 281 matters?
Yes. We provide advisory on trust structure, documentation coordination and Section 281 applications for transactions requiring income tax clearance. Always consult legal counsel for trust deed drafting.
Do you offer online services to clients outside Adilabad?
Yes. Most services are delivered online through document sharing, digital signatures and e-filing. We serve clients across India via WhatsApp, email and digital modes.
